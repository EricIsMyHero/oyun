FIELD MANUAL

Own the paths.
Control the battle.

01

Build your command

Select 8 different unit cards and spells with a combined Charge of no more than 5. Four cards form your hand; deployment cycles in the next card.

02

Place with purpose

Select a card or press 1–4, then click a road or courtyard within 7.5 tiles of your citadel. Units march and fight automatically. Flying units can cross terrain; hovering units follow the ground and resist ground effects.

03

Take the wells

Hold a well for 5 uncontested seconds to capture it. The central well is 17.5 walking tiles from either citadel; outer wells are about 23.23 tiles along their longer routes. Every well you control adds 2% to your mana regeneration. Enemy presence stops capture progress.

04

Spend mana, earn energy

You begin with 12 mana, regenerate 1 every 1.3 seconds, and can hold 20. Spending 18 mana earns 1 spell energy, rounded in steps of 0.05 for each deployment.

05

Cast and cycle

Press Q or select your active spell, then choose its target. A successful cast spends its Energy cost and reveals the next spell in your loadout. Charge is deck capacity, not a consumable.

06

Break the citadel

Both citadels have 1,000 health and a 7.5-tile range. Courtyards leave 1 tile behind and 2.5 ahead, both available for deployment. Destroy the enemy citadel to win. After 5 minutes, surviving citadels enter 2 minutes of double-mana overtime; the healthier citadel then wins.

07

Scout through the fog

Unseen ground stays black. Allied units reveal an 8-tile radius, captured wells reveal 4 tiles, and your citadel reveals 7.5 tiles from the start. Losing sight hides the area again. Enemy units, citadel health and well ownership stay hidden outside your current vision.

08

Move your view

Drag or swipe the battlefield to explore. A short tap places a card; a drag only moves the camera. Click or drag the tactical minimap to jump across the island. Scroll, WASD or arrow keys also move the view. Home or Space returns to your citadel; + and − adjust zoom. Moving the camera never reveals fog.

09

Read the effects

Poison ticks every 0.75 seconds and slows movement by 10%. Burn and Bleeding tick each second; Burn reduces healing by 20%, and Bleeding blocks healing. Predominance clears negative effects and grants immunity. Inspect a visible unit to see active effects and remaining durations.

Faithful to the Codex. 1,000 source points equal 1 tile. A × B means A hits of B damage; deployed Count is separate. Shield Depletion splits incoming damage between shield and health. Missing battle data stays visibly locked.
